A specimen of moving sorts

Doodle Dingbats

The tea is on , the lanterns go up at dusk , and a reply comes back with love .

Hand-drawn glyphs that sit in a line of text at the size of the words around them, move on their own, and do something particular when tapped. Tap any of them. One script, five looks, and the line takes the colour of the text.

On a page

<script src="dingbats.min.js"></script>

<p>Saved <doodle-dingbat name="check"></doodle-dingbat></p>
<i data-dingbat="lantern" data-look="paper"></i>

Either form works; the second survives editors that strip unknown tags. Without a size it is 1em, so it follows the text. Without a label a screen reader skips it as decoration.
